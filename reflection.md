## learning from first AI leasson 
I learned from AI how to solve errors one by one. I also learned how to check the requirements and incorporate them into the project. When it comes to editing, I start with a demo before moving on to personal customization.

At every step, using AI or Copilot was helpful—it felt like having an assistant by my side. The learning patterns were useful, but sometimes they made me feel more confused because I received so many solutions. It also made me eager to keep developing.

The risk is that I'm diving so deeply into AI that I sometimes forget about my own personality. My imagination feels diminished because I'm working so fast.
